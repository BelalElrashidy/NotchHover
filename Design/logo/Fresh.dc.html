Eight different ideas
Not the notch shape. Different metaphors, different palettes.
1 · Aperture
The notch houses the camera. Precise, engineered.
Reads as a photo app.
2 · Peek
An eyelid lifting. Hover to reveal, literally.
Cute; may read as surveillance.
3 · Ribbon
A bookmark hanging from the top edge. Warm, physical.
Bookmarking is not what it does.
4 · Pull
A drawer handle under a lip. Invites the gesture.
Quiet — easy to miss in a Dock.
5 · Keyhole
Everything stays on your machine. States the promise.
Says security app, not utility.
6 · Hover
A panel floating above its own shadow. Names the app.
Generic card shape.
7 · Command
A prompt with a caret. Speaks to the Claude Code side.
Looks like a terminal tool.
8 · Emanate
Ripples spreading from the top edge. Activity, not audio.
Abstract — teaches nothing.
